## Closed execution-admission sequence

For an exact generated consumer entry fact, the package-private service performs one closed sequence:

1. bind a fresh same-verifier claim to the exact consumer fact, current candidate, generation, journal schema,
   recovery cursor, and evidence-session/journal identities;
2. open a new dedicated PostgreSQL session, apply the signed role/settings, and acquire the signed advisory lock;
3. project and bind the connected-session and migration-role authority on that same session;
4. reread the exact durable ledger prefix and predecessor/cumulative catalog, then select the exact next entry and
   its full ordered statement-plan closure;
5. reread the idle session boundary, reproject the migration-role authority, and require canonical equality with
   the original projection;
6. reread the ledger and catalog under the same retained lock, then reread the idle role/lock boundary again;
7. consume the evidence claim only if the same generation/schema/recovery/session/journal boundary remains exact;
8. apply the generated four-pair execution selector and seal a registry-backed, pointer/owner-bound, non-copyable,
   one-shot permit; and
9. consume that permit only through `close_without_mutation`, releasing/resetting/closing the exact session.

The concrete PostgreSQL projection snapshot validates the signed settings and migration role before and after
the final authority projection. The explicit session-boundary checks additionally require idle transaction status,
the migration role, and the same advisory lock. No transaction-opening or mutation operation exists on the permit.

## Authority and cleanup invariants

- A new execution claim cannot be reconstructed from the ordinary consumer fact or the immutable ADR-0021 permit.
- Claim, terminal use record, evidence boundary, and permit each bind all five generated profile identities:
  preflight, consumer, ADR-0021 admission, execution admission, and success writer.
- The permit binds the selected migration/entry/plan closure, ledger rows/head/length/digest, authority projections,
  catalog projection/contract, database identity, advisory key, generation, and consumed evidence boundary.
- Literal, copied, swapped, stale, foreign-verifier, registry-missing, second-use, or closed-evidence authority fails
  closed. Evidence-session close revokes every remaining claim/use record.
- Once a fact enters execution admission, its evidence-bound terminal use record prevents a second attempt even if
  selection, sealing, or cleanup subsequently fails.
- Unlock/reset/close ambiguity dominates the prospective `NOT_IMPLEMENTED` result; cleanup still targets the exact
  retained session and advisory key after permit tamper or evidence revocation.
